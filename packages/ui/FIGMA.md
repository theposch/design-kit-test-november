# Figma tracker

What has been built from Figma, what was decided along the way, and what was left out on purpose. Kept up to date by
the `figma-to-code` skill. Snapshots and maps live in `figma/specs/`, Figma pictures in `figma/baselines/`.

Status: `todo` · `in progress` · `blocked` (with why) · `done` (clean parity table in both modes) · `skipped`
(see below).
